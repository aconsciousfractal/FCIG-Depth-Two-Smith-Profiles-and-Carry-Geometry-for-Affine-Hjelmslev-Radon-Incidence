# Public claim boundary

## Affirmative mathematical statements

The paper proves, for every prime `p`, the complete `p`-primary Smith profile
of the depth-two affine point--line incidence matrix stated in its abstract.
It also proves the depth-two transfer extension is nonsplit and gives an exact
relative two-chart presentation with a typed cross-chart class and a
compatible carry representative.

The proof of the displayed affine integer profile is written in the paper and
does not identify a projective incidence matrix with the affine matrix. The
finite companion provides supporting computational cross-checks only.

## Explicit nonclaims

This package does not claim:

- novelty, firstness or priority for any statement;
- a literature-wide or exhaustive source search;
- a closed Smith profile at every depth;
- a full-matrix companion computation or finite certificate at
  `(p,n)=(5,2)`; the all-prime theorem itself includes `p=5`;
- that finite calculations prove an all-prime theorem;
- that the two-chart carry admits a general compression theorem;
- that adjacent projective modular-rank results determine the affine integer
  Smith profile;
- tagged-PDF accessibility for the shipped PDF.

## Computational scope

The companion exactly reproduces the bounded cases `(2,2)`, `(3,2)` and
`(2,3)`, plus top-shell `(2,4)` obstruction data, a deterministic 1600-case
local-index/HNF property suite, and two complete-lattice HNF comparisons. The
Bockstein values `u_2=1` and `u_3=0` are declared manuscript controls; the
appendix, not the program, proves their all-prime law.

Readers should cite the paper for mathematical statements and describe the
software only as exact reproducibility and mutation-sensitive cross-checking.

The mathematical-development provenance and author-responsibility boundary
are stated in `AI_USE.md` and in the paper itself.
