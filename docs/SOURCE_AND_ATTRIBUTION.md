# Source and attribution boundary

The exact bibliography is `paper/references.bib`. No cited paper, database export or
third-party source tree is redistributed in this package.

## Imported ingredients

- Weber and Künzer supply the cyclic Wedderburn diagonal elementary-divisor
  formulas used for the two diagonal chart blocks.
- Lipman and Lipman--Watanabe supply the complete-ideal/antinef and
  Hoskin--Deligne colength machinery used in the modular-rank proof.
- Stacks Project tag `00M0` supplies the standard long exact Tor sequence.
- Standard Smith-form, cyclotomic and module-extension ingredients are cited
  at their points of use.

The paper states the exact proposition, section or lemma locator whenever an
external theorem is invoked. Imported results remain the work of their
authors and are not rebranded as contributions of this package.

## Adjacent, nontransferred literature

Łaba--Trainor and Dvir concern the modular incidence object and rank bounds;
their work is not used to pass from a modular rank to the integral Smith
profile. Landjev, Bajalan--Landjev--Rousseva and the broader projective
Hjelmslev literature concern adjacent projective settings; no projective
matrix is identified with the affine matrix studied here.

For Landjev 2017 and Bajalan--Landjev--Rousseva 2023, the available boundary
was bibliographic metadata and abstract-level scope. No theorem text from
those records is imported. This limited access is a ceiling on originality
statements, not evidence of novelty.

## Parts proved in the manuscript

The affine two-chart assembly, its explicit cross block, the compatible
clipped-line carry and the complete depth-two affine integer profile are
proved in the manuscript. The code reconstructs finite controls for these
objects but is not the proof.

## Stable locators

DOIs and stable public locators are recorded in `paper/references.bib`. Entries with
no editorial DOI retain an author, institutional or official-project locator;
absence of a DOI is not filled by conjecture. No source is copied merely
because it was accessible.
